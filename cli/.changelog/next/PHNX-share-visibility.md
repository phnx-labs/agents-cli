- **`agents artifacts share visibility <target> <level>` changes a published page's visibility in place.** Until now the only way to re-scope an already-shared page (public ↔ unlisted ↔ me ↔ org) was a full re-publish or a delete. The new verb re-stamps only the visibility on the stored object: the slug — and so the URL — is preserved, the body is untouched, and (like `agents artifacts share edit`) it is a metadata-only rewrite that creates no revision. It reuses the delete target parser (full URL, `<user>/<slug>`, or a bare slug in your namespace) and the same `PATCH` metadata-edit route/client — visibility is a first-class edit field alongside `label`, never a `--meta` entry (`visibility` is reserved). `me`/`org` require a Phoenix session and fail loud with an `agents auth login` hint when signed out; `org` is refused on a public-inbox email domain. A BYO endpoint whose deployed Worker predates the visibility edit fails loud (`agents artifacts share update`) rather than silently reporting success. **Adds a `visibility` field to the Worker `PATCH` route, so already-provisioned BYO endpoints must run `agents artifacts share update` to pick it up (their template reads `outdated` until then); the managed endpoint is updated by the release.** Source: `cli/src/commands/share.ts`, `cli/src/lib/share/worker-template.ts`, `cli/src/lib/share/publish.ts`.
