- **A non-ASCII share title / `--label` / `--meta` value now publishes in full instead of degrading to `(unnamed)` or dropping glyphs (PHNX-2786).** `publishToEndpoint` still sends the latin1-safe folded value in `x-share-<field>` (so an already-deployed Worker keeps rendering exactly as before — the ByteString crash stays fixed), and now ALSO sends a percent-encoded `x-share-<field>-u` companion plus an `x-share-encoding: percent` opt-in whenever the fold was lossy. An updated Worker (`agents artifacts share update`) prefers the decoded companion, so a Japanese/Arabic/Hindi title or an emoji renders as written; a Worker that predates the scheme ignores the unknown headers and folds gracefully. `--meta` rides its whole raw object in one percent-encoded `x-share-meta-u` companion. Source: `cli/src/lib/share/publish.ts`, `cli/src/lib/share/worker-template.ts`.
