- **Daemon supervisor hardening: abandon hung ticks, thread an AbortSignal, and bring account-state/catch-up under the supervisor (PHNX-3608).** A supervised tick that awaited something that never settled used to park the service but hold its in-flight guard forever, so the backoff restart, `agents daemon services restart`, and SIGHUP reload were all blocked — a service could freeze for the daemon's life while still looking healthy (the 12h usage-dark class). On a deadline breach the supervisor now ABANDONS the tick: it aborts the tick's new `AbortSignal`, releases the in-flight guard immediately, and schedules the backoff restart right away, draining the runaway promise separately. `PeriodicService.tick(ctx, signal)` receives that signal so a service can bound its own I/O. `start()`/`stop()`/`restart()` are now bounded by a lifecycle deadline so a wedged bind/close can't stall daemon startup or shutdown. The account-state usage/auth refresh — previously its own un-deadlined `setInterval` behind a `usageRunning` latch (the exact hang that froze usage) — is now a supervised `PeriodicService` with a real deadline + abort, and catch-up recovery moved off its bare `setInterval` onto a supervised `catchup` service. Disabling the `monitors` service now routes through `supervisor.stop`, and a stopped monitor engine dispatches nothing on tick — so a disabled monitors service actually stops firing instead of being ticked with its last-loaded set.
