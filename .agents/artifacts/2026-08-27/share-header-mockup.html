Share page — attribution header

A Google-Drive-style bar at the top of every shared artifact: who shared it, what made it, when, how many views, and — the part Rush is missing — an instant visual cue of who can see it. What's shown adapts to the visibility tier.

Design language stays agents-cli (dark, lime accent). We only borrow the useful information from Rush's share page (author, "made with", how-it-was-made, timestamp, views). Names are masked; the email is never shown.

The owner viewing their own private page. Shows your own full name and an inline control to change who can see it. Amber lock = private, read instantly.
