- **Auto-sync the reserved `auth` bundle across the fleet (PHNX-2371).**
  File-backend `secrets export --device` no longer forwards
  `AGENTS_SECRETS_PASSPHRASE` (that keyed remotes to a secret their daemons
  lacked while still printing "Imported N key(s)"). Import and remote push
  now read-back-verify decryptability. The daemon `auth-sync` service, plus
  `fleet apply` and `repo push/pull user`, push a local file-backed `auth`
  bundle to pinned devices that lack it. Source: `cli/src/lib/secrets/reserved-sync.ts`,
  `cli/src/lib/secrets/remote.ts`, `cli/src/lib/daemon/auth-sync-service.ts`.
