01Why the extension's picker dies

Side by side, the two selection engines. The CLI's is strict where it matters and the extension's is blind exactly there:

ConcernCLI balanced (rotate.ts)Factory pickBestVersion
Weekly windowexcluded when maxed — ANY blocking window counts (hasUsageAvailable, rotate.ts:145)ignored — ranks on the 5-hour window only (resumeInBest.ts:28)
Stale usage datadistrusted past 5 min (USAGE_DECISION_MAX_AGE_MS, rotate.ts:130)no freshness check
Zero healthy accountsreturns null (rotate.ts:305)"better somewhere than nowhere" — rotates into the exhausted account (resumeInBest.ts:205-208)
Who picks the accountweighted-random by remaining headroom (pickBalancedCandidate, rotate.ts:291)pins a binary, CLI never consulted
the one CLI gap Zero healthy accounts under balanced/available currently warns "found no usable version; falling back to defaults" and launches the pinned default anyway (exec.ts:2265-2268), which sits on the same weekly-limited account. With that fallback, even a pure balanced rotate still loops. Step 1 makes the CLI fail loudly instead; the rest of the plan stands on it.

  • The CLI's new zero-healthy error text joins the watchdog's tail detection (watchdog.ts:34): treated as rate-limited with cooldown to the parsed reset → one kind:'rotate' skip event per cooldown, no re-rotate.
