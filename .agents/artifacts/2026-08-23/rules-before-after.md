## Story

Here is the whole rule layer, before and after. The cut is **51%** — from 17,255 words to 8,412 (23.0k → 11.2k tokens injected into every session). Three things happen to a rule and nothing else: it stays untouched, it gets slimmed, or it leaves the rule layer (its content moves into a skill/hook you already have, or it was a duplicate and gets deleted). No hard rule, recipe, or guard is dropped — the procedures just live where you load them instead of in front of every agent all the time.
