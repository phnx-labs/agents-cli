- **The menu-bar helper no longer permanently forgets refresh children after a crash (PHNX-2413).** Child ownership is persisted before spawn in a versioned, cross-process-locked registry, completed with PID/PGID/start-time/executable/argv identity, and recoverable from an unguessable provenance marker carried by the group-leading supervisor if the helper dies in the spawn-to-registration window. Reaping now retains individual ambiguous entries and registry I/O failures for retry, and deletes a child only after its process group is confirmed absent. Existing two-field records migrate conservatively. Source: `cli/menubar/Sources/MenubarHelper/ChildProcess.swift`.
