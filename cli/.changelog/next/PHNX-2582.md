- **Raise SSH `ControlPersist` to 10 minutes so fleet polls reuse warm connections (PHNX-2582).** The OpenSSH multiplex master now lives for 10 minutes after its last use (`SSH_CONTROL_PERSIST_SECONDS`) instead of 60 seconds. The master only survives while it stays idle under that window, and the dominant repeating fleet touch — the daemon's 5-minute service loop — was longer than the old 60 s persist, so every poll paid a fresh TCP+auth handshake (~100-300 ms direct, up to ~500 ms relayed). 10 minutes clears the 5-minute cadence with margin, so a periodic `--device`/fan-out poll lands on a still-warm master (~3-5 ms) instead. The window stays bounded so an idle master to a slept host is reaped rather than lingering indefinitely. Source: `cli/src/lib/ssh-exec.ts`.
