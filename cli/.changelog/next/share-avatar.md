- **The share attribution bar now always shows the sharer's avatar.** Every shared page (and every asset viewer page) renders an avatar in the bar next to "Shared by …": a coloured initials circle derived from the handle, always present, so a share is never faceless — especially useful for an org/company share where knowing *who* posted it matters. When the sharer is signed in, the CLI stamps a Gravatar (keyed on a SHA-256 of the lowercased email — only the hash lands in public metadata, never the raw address) so a real profile photo layers over the initials; if the user has no Gravatar the photo request 404s and the bar cleanly falls back to the initials circle (`onerror`, honoured because the share page sets no CSP). Source: `cli/src/lib/share/worker-template.ts` (render + `x-share-avatar` → `customMetadata.avatar`), `cli/src/lib/share/publish.ts` (`resolveShareAvatar`). Deployed by re-running `agents artifacts share update`; a hosted-photo pipeline for users without a Gravatar is tracked as a follow-up.
